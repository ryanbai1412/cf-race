## Independent algorithms and constraints

- 489C: exactly one line, m in 1..100 and s in 0..900. Impossibility is s>9m or zero sum with m>1. For the minimum reserve leading 1 and allocate the remaining sum from right to left; for the maximum allocate 9 greedily left to right. Positional weights prove lexicographic optimality. Exhaustively enumerate integer candidates for m=1..4 and s=0..9m+2 (102 cases).
- 710B: exactly n in 1..300000 and n coordinates in [-1e9,1e9] on the next line. Independently select order statistic floor((n-1)/2) by partitioning, rather than importing sorted-median reference code. The L1 objective's minimizer interval has that left endpoint. Enumerate all multisets of sizes 1..6 over -2..2 and brute every integer position (461 cases).
- 757B: exactly n in 1..100000 and n strengths in 1..100000. Independently trial-factor each distinct strength, count multiplicities for each prime, and take max(1,counts). Any nontrivial gcd contains a prime; any such prime's divisible values form a valid subset. Exhaustively enumerate all multisets of sizes 1..6 over 1..6 and all subsets (923 cases). A singleton is legal even when its strength is 1.
- 485A: exactly one line, a,m in 1..100000. Track residues r=a mod m until zero or repetition; the transition is r->2r mod m. An independent literal a+=a%m brute was checked exhaustively for a,m in 1..150 (22,500 cases).
- 505B: n in 2..100; m in 1..100; exactly m edges with 1<=a<b<=n and 1<=c<=m, no repeated (a,b,c); q in 1..100 and q distinct-endpoint queries with endpoints in 1..n. Parallel pairs in different colors are permitted; connectivity is not promised. Independently BFS/DFS each color graph and query. Compared with explicit simple-path enumeration on 500 seeded small graphs and every ordered distinct-endpoint query.
- 507B: exactly one line, r in 1..100000, all four coordinates in [-100000,100000]. Each rotation can move the center at most 2r in any direction; every displacement up to that is attainable, so the answer is the least k with (2rk)^2 >= squared distance. Integer binary search avoids floating-point rounding. Linear search checked r=1..7 and displacement coordinates -10..10 (3,087 cases).
