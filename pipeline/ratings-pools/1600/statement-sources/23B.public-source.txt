B. Party
time limit per test
2 seconds
memory limit per test
256 megabytes
input
stdin
output
stdout

n people came to a party. Then those, who had no friends among people at the party, left. Then
those, who had exactly 1 friend among those who stayed, left as well. Then those, who had exactly 2,
3, ..., n - 1 friends among those who stayed by the moment of their leaving, did the same.

What is the maximum amount of people that could stay at the party in the end?

Input

The first input line contains one number t — amount of tests (1 ≤ t ≤ 10⁵). Each of the following t
lines contains one integer number n (1 ≤ n ≤ 10⁵).

Output

For each test output in a separate line one number — the maximum amount of people that could stay in
the end.

Examples
Input

1
3

Output

1
